# Audit Readiness Score

This document explains how readiness scoring should be interpreted inside the broader audit-readiness model.

## What The Score Represents

An audit readiness score is a compressed management view of current completeness across the major governance layers.

Typical input areas include:

- inventory completeness
- classification completeness
- documentation and evidence completeness
- control coverage
- governance review status
- open remediation gaps

## Why It Matters

A score should not replace the underlying records. It should summarize whether the system is broadly ready, partially ready, or materially incomplete based on the actual data already present in the governance workflow.

## Practical Use

The score is most useful when it is:

- automatically derived from current records
- broken down by layer
- linked to open gaps and next actions
- visible in management and audit-preparation contexts
